Who we sell to
Three launch segments, South Africa first
Gauteng pilot, then national, before Nigeria, Kenya and Ghana.
Segment 1
Solo entrepreneurs
Who
Founder-run businesses of 1–5 people: side hustles, beauty and braiding, fashion resellers, home bakers, coaches, informal retailers selling on Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp
Main tier
Starter R499 → Growth
Pain
No time or budget for a marketer; posts inconsistently; loses sales in WhatsApp DMs; payment links drop off

“Your AI marketing team for R499/mo, and you sell right inside WhatsApp”
Buying motion
Self-serve: 14-day trial → Founding Member offer
Segment 2
SMEs
Who

Main tier
Growth R1,999 → Scale R4,999

6+ disconnected tools; agency retainers of R15k+/mo; no attribution from social to sales; WhatsApp handled manually by staff
Promise
“Replace the tool stack, sell and get paid in chat, see what drives revenue”
Buying motion
Trial + guided onboarding call; proposal for multi-brand / Scale
Segment 3
Agencies
Who
Marketing, digital and social media agencies and freelancers managing 5–50 SMB clients
Main tier
Partner R5,599 /mo, 30% off Agency
Pain
Margin squeeze; manual reporting; too many tools per client; clients want WhatsApp commerce and AI
Promise
“Serve more clients under your brand: set your own retail price and keep the spread”
Buying motion
Partner programme: demo → pilot client → partner wholesale plan, reseller billing

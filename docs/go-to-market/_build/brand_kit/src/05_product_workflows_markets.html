Market entry sequence

  1. South Africa

    Home market. Gauteng pilot, then national. Launch window 1 Dec 2026 – 31 Jan 2027.

  2. Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana

    First expansion wave. All three covered by Paystack, pawaPay and Fincra; priced in NGN, KES, GHS.

  3. Rest of the 23

    Self-serve in USD: Côte d'Ivoire, Rwanda, Uganda, Tanzania, Zambia, francophone West and Central Africa and more.

  4. Botswana, Namibia

    Coming soon, once a rail covers them. Waitlist only.

Gating rule

Prices and checkout appear only in rail-covered countries. Everywhere else, including Botswana, Namibia and all countries outside Africa, offer a waitlist, never a price or a buy button. South Sudan and Zimbabwe are payout-only via Fincra: treat them as gated until Fincra confirms subscription collection.

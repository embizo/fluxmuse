Market entry sequence

  1. South Africa

    Open now. A small first group of businesses, set up by hand, then national. Founding Member pricing, while it lasts.

  2. Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana

    Priced in NGN, KES and GHS. Not on sale until Fincra and pawaPay accounts are live and checkout is tested.

  3. Other covered markets

    Self-serve in USD, later.

  4. Botswana, Namibia

    Coming soon, once a provider covers them. Waitlist only.

Gating rule

Prices and checkout appear only where paid checkout is open: South Africa today. Everywhere else, offer a waitlist, never a price or a buy button. No launch dates for other markets until the founder sets them.

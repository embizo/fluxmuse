Use for

Segment or category tags, secondary chart series, small illustration details, the 8-colour stripe as a brand signature.

Don't use for

Body text, buttons, backgrounds behind copy, or as a rainbow gradient across a whole layout.

Watch out

Red and Green also read as error and success. Don't use them as neutral category colours next to status indicators.

Accessibility rules

Contrast measured with the WCAG 2.x formula. AA needs 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text (24 px, or 18.7 px bold).

Rule

White on Flux Orange is 2.9:1 and fails AA, even for large text. Put Ink or Night text on orange buttons and fills. For orange text or links on white, use Deep Orange #C24E00. Keep Flux Orange for fills, icons, accents and large display type on Night.

Usage

  • Poppins for headlines, big numbers and short labels. Never for paragraphs.
  • Inter for body, UI, tables and captions.
  • Two weights per layout is usually enough.

Settings

  • Sentence case headlines, text-wrap: balance.
  • Tabular figures for prices and tables.
  • Eyebrows only in caps, with letter-spacing.

Fallbacks

  • Poppins → Avenir Next → Segoe UI → Arial.
  • Inter → system UI → Arial.
  • Email and Office documents: Arial.

In the product: system fonts

The FluxMuse app uses the device's system UI font for speed and native feel. Screenshots will look like this, not like Inter. That's expected; don't retouch them.

08

Imagery & graphic motifs

Real businesses, phones in hand

We have no brand photography yet. Until the pilot shoot, use product composites and infographics, and brief photographers with the direction below.

Photography direction

  • Real African SMB owners and teams, photographed where they work: salons and braiding studios, markets and spaza shops, home kitchens and bakeries, workshops, design studios, clinics.
  • Phones and WhatsApp in hand. Show the moment of work: replying to a customer, packing an order, showing a before-and-after, tapping to pay.
  • Natural light, warm but true skin tones, real clutter kept in. Shallow depth of field so screens and faces read.
  • Candid over posed. People looking at their work or their customer, not at the camera with a thumbs up.
  • Specific places: Gauteng first (Soweto, Tembisa, Mamelodi, Sandton), then Lagos, Nairobi, Accra. Caption the city.
  • Consent always. Signed model releases; blur customer names and numbers on any screen in frame (POPIA).

Avoid

Stock clichés

Handshakes in glass offices, headset call-centre smiles, people pointing at floating holograms, glowing robot brains.

"Africa" shorthand

Savannah sunsets, safari animals, generic "tribal" patterns, poverty imagery. We show modern commerce.

Fake or AI-generated people

No generated faces presented as customers, and no staged "customer" photos with invented names or results.

Motif

The node network

Borrowed from the Muse icon. Thin lines, small nodes, one orange path at most.

Node network rules

  • 1–1.5 px lines at 8–14% opacity (white on Night, Slate on light).
  • Corners and edges only; keep it out from behind text and faces.
  • At most one Flux Orange path, to hint at "the connection that made the sale".
  • Never animate it into a spinning globe or a brain.

Iconography

Lucide-style outline icons on a 24 px grid: 2 px stroke, round caps and joins, no fills. Colour with Ink, Slate or Deep Orange; on Night use white or Glow.

Illustration don'ts

  • No 3D blobs, isometric cities or robot mascots.
  • No mixing filled and outline icon sets in one layout.
  • No payment-provider or platform logos as icons (write the name instead).
  • No rainbow gradients built from the Muse spectrum.
  • Don't redraw or animate the Muse head as a character.
09

Data visualisation & infographics

Label it, source it, keep it honest

Charts and infographics carry our facts to investors and partners, so they follow stricter rules than anything else in the kit.

Chart style rules

  • Series order: Flux Orange for the series that matters, then Slate, then spectrum accents in the order below. Five series maximum.
  • Direct labels at the end of lines or on bars. Avoid legends when you can.
  • Money in R m for the model and investor materials: "R12.4m", one decimal. Local currency in market slides; label FX assumptions.
  • Zero baseline for bars. Hairline gridlines in Mist or Night-2. No 3D, no shadows, no pie charts above 4 slices.
  • Label every assumption "est." or "indicative", with a source line under the chart.
  • Not live? Dash it. Channels in Meta review (e.g. Instagram) use a dashed outline with an "in review" tag.
  • Figures from the financial model only. Never draw a chart from a number that isn't in the model or the facts file.

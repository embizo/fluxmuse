Icon only: the Muse

Use where the wordmark won't fit: avatars, app icons, favicons, watermarks.

What the Muse means

The icon is a head built from low-poly facets in the full Muse spectrum, resting on a fine node network.

The facets are the many voices a business speaks with: languages, channels, ideas. The network is the AI team underneath, connecting every conversation to a result. The head keeps a person at the centre: the owner's creativity, amplified rather than replaced.

This is how we describe the mark in brand materials. It is an interpretation, not a design brief for new illustrations.

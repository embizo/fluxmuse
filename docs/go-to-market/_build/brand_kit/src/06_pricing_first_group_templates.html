Claims rules
  • Proof we can show today: verified Meta Tech Provider; a live demo on the prospect's own products; a real founder who sets them up.
  • Never: customer counts, ratings, testimonials, results, "trusted by", "proven", "pilot brands" or uplift numbers.

  • No business names, logos or photos without signed consent.

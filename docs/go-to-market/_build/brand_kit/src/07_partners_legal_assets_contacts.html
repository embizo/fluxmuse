Partner & reseller usage

Your brand in front. FluxMuse behind.

Agencies on the Agency tier can white-label FluxMuse for their clients. These rules decide when our brand shows and how.

White-label

Client-facing product

Use your own logo, colours and domain. Don't show the FluxMuse logo inside a white-labelled workspace, and don't imply clients have a contract with FluxMuse.

Powered by FluxMuse

Optional credit line

Where you want to credit the platform (footers, proposals, reports), write "Powered by FluxMuse" as text in your body font. Link to fluxmuse.ai if you like.

Co-branded

Joint marketing

For co-marketing, webinars and the partner directory, use the co-branding lockup below. Needs written approval from FluxMuse for each campaign.

PARTNER LOGO
  • Partner logo left, FluxMuse right, separated by a 1 px divider in Slate or Night-2.
  • Equal optical height; FluxMuse is never larger than the partner's logo.
  • Clear space of x around the whole lockup and between each logo and the divider.
  • Use the dark logo on Night until the light "F" is fixed.
  • Never merge the logos, recolour ours to your palette, or add "certified", "official" or "endorsed" without a written agreement.
Partners may

Set their own client prices · use FluxMuse name and screenshots from the approved list · describe features exactly as in this kit · list in the partner directory.

Partners may not

Advertise the Founding Member offer as their own discount · quote results from the pilot or other clients as FluxMuse results · claim Meta permissions or payment rails in their own name · register domains or handles containing "fluxmuse".

Pricing

Partners buy the Agency tier at the wholesale price of R5,599/mo (30% off list; ₦463,000 · KSh 44,999 · GH₵ 3,799 · US$299) and set their own retail prices. When showing partner economics, use the illustrative example only and label it: 20 clients × R1,500 = R30,000/mo, minus R5,599 = R24,401/mo gross spread before the partner's own costs. Never quote a fixed margin percentage. Referral commission, directory criteria and co-marketing terms: [[PARTNER AGREEMENT TERMS]].

Legal & compliance language

Lines to copy, claims to avoid

Standard wording for footers, decks and consent screens. Have legal counsel review before first external use.

Trademark line

FluxMuse and the Muse icon are trademarks of Fluxmuse Pty Ltd. WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram and Messenger are trademarks of Meta Platforms, Inc. Other product and company names are the property of their respective owners.

Use ™/® only once registration status is confirmed: [[TM STATUS]]

Meta permissions claim rule

Approved, OK to describe as live: Pages list, Page metadata, Page messaging (Messenger), WhatsApp Business messaging and management, public profile.

In App Review, never claim as live: Page posting and engagement insights, Instagram publishing, comments and DMs, business management.

Say "verified Meta Tech Provider". Don't say "Meta partner", "Meta certified" or "official WhatsApp partner".

WhatsApp opt-in consent (POPIA)

[ ] Yes, [[BUSINESS NAME]] may send me order updates and offers on WhatsApp at the number above. I can reply STOP at any time to opt out. Read how we use your information: [[PRIVACY NOTICE LINK]]

Unticked by default. Record the date, source and wording of each opt-in. Separate consent for marketing vs order updates where required.

Payment provider names

  • Write provider names as plain text: Yoco, Ozow, Paystack, pawaPay, Fincra, spelled with their own capitalisation.
  • No provider logos unless you follow that provider's brand guidelines and have the current files.
  • Say "payments powered by local rails like Yoco and Ozow", not "Yoco partner" or "backed by Paystack".
  • Never list SnapScan, Stitch, Flutterwave, PayFast or M-Pesa direct.
Also

"POPIA, NDPR and GDPR ready" is the approved phrasing, not "compliant" or "certified". Market-size figures are estimates: label them "est." with the source. Don't repeat the "40–60% reseller margins" line from the /for-agencies page; partner economics are always shown as a labelled illustrative example.

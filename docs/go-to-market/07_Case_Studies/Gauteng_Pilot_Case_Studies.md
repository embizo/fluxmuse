## Lead case study: "The Gauteng braiding studio"

**Why this one:** of all the pilot archetypes, this is the most attainable proof point with the lowest barrier to entry.

| Criterion | Why a township/suburban braiding & hair studio scores high |
|---|---|
| Access | Owner-operator decides alone: no procurement, no IT department, no board. Reachable through walk-ins, community WhatsApp groups and word of mouth in Soweto, Tembisa, Alexandra, Mamelodi, Soshanguve. |
| Already on our channels | Books clients and shows work on WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook today, so no behaviour change is needed. |
| Fits the product | Visual before/after content (Creator), bookings and reminders in WhatsApp (chatbot/Flows), deposits and product sales (braiding hair, extensions, aftercare) through Yoco card / Ozow EFT, repeat-visit loyalty. |
| Fast, measurable results | Short booking cycles (weekly/monthly), so 60 days is enough to show bookings, deposits collected, no-show reduction and repeat customers. |
| Multilingual | Content in isiZulu, Sesotho and English shows off a real differentiator. |
| Segment | **Solo entrepreneur → Starter (R499)**, with a natural upgrade to Growth for catalog + checkout. |
| Replicable | Thousands of similar studios across Gauteng. The story sells directly to our biggest launch segment. |

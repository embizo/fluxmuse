# First group of businesses: case study framework

**Status: FRAMEWORK. Contains no real results, and there are no case studies yet.** (Refreshed 2026-10-05.)

FluxMuse has no paying customers and no pilot cohort. We are opening with a small first group of South African
businesses, and the founder sets each one up by hand. This file is how we will record what happens for them, so that a
case study can be written later, honestly, and only with the business's written permission.

Source of truth for prices, offer and capability status: `../08_Prospects/CURRENT_OFFER.md`. Voice and allowed claims:
`../08_Flux_Loop/FluxMuse_Taste_Profile.md`.

---

## Rules (read before anything goes out)

1. **Consent first.** No business name, logo, photo, quote or screenshot is used anywhere without signed written consent
   for that specific use. Consent can be withdrawn; if it is, remove the material.
2. **Measured, never projected.** A case study reports what actually happened, over a stated period, with how each number
   was measured. Goals agreed at kickoff are goals. They are never shown as results, and never on the same line as a
   result without the word "goal".
3. **No aggregates from a handful.** Don't publish averages or totals across businesses ("across our businesses...") until
   there is enough data to be meaningful and the founder signs off. `[[FOUNDER TO CONFIRM: minimum number of businesses]]`
4. **Label every capability honestly.** Use the three labels from CURRENT_OFFER.md §3: live (newly launched), in setup
   (being switched on), not available. Don't credit a result to a feature that was not live for that business.
5. **POPIA.** Blur customer names and phone numbers in every screenshot. Broadcasts only to consented contacts.
6. **Never write** "pilot", "pilot brands", "our customers", "trusted by", "proven", uplift numbers, "10x" or any
   guarantee of sales. The lead guarantee may be mentioned only with its conditions.
7. **Until a real case study exists,** materials say "case studies to follow, with each business's permission". Never
   show a filled-in example with invented numbers.

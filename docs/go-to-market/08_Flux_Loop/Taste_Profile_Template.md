# Taste Profile: onboarding template

**Status: TEMPLATE.** Make one copy per business we onboard (`Taste_Profile_[[BRAND]].md`)
and keep the copies in the brand's private workspace folder, **not** in this repo.
They hold the owner's words and customer details.

**What it is.** A short, living write-up of what makes this business *this*
business. It describes who buys, how they talk, which stories are true, and
what makes people stop scrolling. Every piece of content, ad and chatbot reply
FluxMuse writes for the brand starts from it. It's the first deliverable of
onboarding and it changes after every sprint. Background:
`Flux_Loop_Refinement.md` §3.

**Who fills it in.** The FluxMuse onboarding lead, on a 30–45 minute call or
over WhatsApp voice notes, in the owner's language. Write down the owner's
**own words** wherever you can. The aim is their voice, not a polished summary.

**Rules**
- Only use customer chats, photos or reviews with the customer's permission
  and in line with POPIA. Remove customer names and numbers.
- Write down claims only if they're true and the owner can stand behind them
  (no "best in Gauteng" unless they can show it).
- Leave a blank as `[[ ]]` rather than guessing. Gaps get filled in later sprints.

## Part A: interview guide (10 questions)

Ask in order. Follow-ups in *italics*. Most questions map to one profile field.

| # | Question | Fills |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tell me about your best customer, the one you wish you had 50 more of. | B1 |
| 2 | How do customers usually first hear about you, and where do they message you? | B1, B4 |
| 3 | What do customers ask you most often before they buy? *What makes them hesitate?* | B2, B3 |
| 4 | Read me (or forward) a few messages from happy customers. *What words do they use?* | B2 |
| 5 | Why did you start this business? *What's the story you tell friends?* | C1 |
| 6 | What do you do that others nearby don't? *What would customers miss if you closed?* | C2, C3 |
| 7 | Why should someone book or buy *now*, this month? *Season, event, stock, price?* | C4 |
| 8 | Show me 2–3 posts that did really well, and 1 that flopped. *Why do you think?* | D1, D2 |
| 9 | Which languages do you and your customers use? *Do you mix them? Any slang you love or hate?* | E1, E2 |
| 10 | Is there anything you'd never want us to say or post? | E4 |

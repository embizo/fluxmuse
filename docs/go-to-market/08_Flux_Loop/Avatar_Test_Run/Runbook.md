# Avatar video test: runbook

**Owner:** Thabo Malebadi · **Kit:** `../Avatar_Video_Test_Kit.md` ·
**Sheet:** `Avatar_Test_Scoring.xlsx`

**Length:** about 18 working days, longer than the kit's "2 weeks". Three
posts, 3 days apart, plus 72 hours of measuring after the last one, don't fit
in 14 days. Plan for it now rather than cut the measuring short.

**Before day 1:**
- Finish the FluxMuse dry run (`FluxMuse_Dry_Run.md`).
- Set a budget for tools: `[[R ]]`.
- Fill in the cost limit for the cost gate on the sheet's **Gates** tab.

---

## Week 1: recruit, check voices, make videos

### Day 1: voice check (no businesses needed yet)
1. Generate the voice-check script (`Production_Prompts.md` §2) in South
   African English and Afrikaans with each voice tool you're trying.
2. Ask a fluent Afrikaans speaker to listen and rate each clip: **natural /
   OK / poor / not available**. Record it on the sheet's **Voice check** tab.
3. If no tool sounds natural in Afrikaans, note it on the sheet. That result
   already settles the Local voice gate, so carry on in English only.
   (isiZulu, Sesotho and other languages are deferred to later sprints.)

### Days 1–4: recruit 6–10 businesses
- **Mix:**
  - at least 3 solo businesses (beauty, fashion, food);
  - at least 2 small businesses with staff;
  - at least 2 whose customers mostly speak Afrikaans;
  - at least 1 owner who already films themselves.
- **Every business must already post** on WhatsApp Status or Facebook at least
  weekly.
- **Leave out regulated categories:** financial products, insurance, property,
  health claims, alcohol, gambling, weight loss.
- **Approach them one to one** through a channel the business publishes
  itself, or through a warm introduction. Never guess or scrape a number.

**First message (send by hand, edit for each business):**

> Hi `[[NAME]]`, I'm Thabo, founder of FluxMuse, a South African company that helps small businesses market and sell on WhatsApp. I like what you're doing with `[[BUSINESS]]` (`[[one real detail from their page]]`). We're testing a few short video styles made from a business's own product photos, and I'd love to make three for you, at no cost, for you to keep or bin. Would a 10-minute chat this week suit you? If you'd rather not hear from me, just say so and I won't message again.

**Consent message, once they say yes (they reply YES):**

> Thanks `[[NAME]]`. Before I start, can you confirm by replying YES that: (1) I can use your `[[PRODUCT]]` photos to make 3 short videos for `[[BUSINESS]]`, one of which uses an AI presenter; (2) you choose whether to post any of them, with no pressure; (3) I can share the results with my team without your name. You can change your mind at any time.

**Then:**
- Add the business to the **Brands** tab, with the consent date.
- Agree which product and offer to use. It must be one product, a real price,
  and only a real offer.
- Ask for 5–10 product photos.
- Ask the owner who said they'll film for a 10-second selfie: hook plus call
  to action, holding the phone vertical, in good light.

### Days 3–7: make the videos
For each business:
1. **Script:** write it from the skeleton in the kit (§2), using their Taste
   Profile if they have one. Otherwise do a 15-minute version of the Taste
   Profile interview first.
2. **Make three videos.** Their order and the setup are in the kit §2 and
   `Production_Prompts.md`:
   - **A:** avatar presenter;
   - **B:** product video with a voiceover;
   - **C:** the owner's selfie.

   Each video is vertical 9:16, 20–30 seconds, with burned-in captions.
   Format A carries an "AI presenter" label in the corner the whole time.
3. **Log every attempt** on the **Videos** tab: tool, cost in rand, number of
   attempts, staff minutes and what went wrong. Score format A against the
   quality bar (kit §6).
4. **Choose an avatar** from the roster. No avatar may be used for more than 2
   businesses.
5. **Make the tracking link and code** for each video (`Tracking_Codes.md`).
   The **Tracking** tab builds them for you.

## Week 2: owner review and posting

### Day 8: show the owner
Send all three videos together, labelled **1, 2, 3 in a random order**. Use
the order generated on the **Brands** tab so it isn't always A first. Ask the
questions by call or by voice note, and write the answers in their own words:

1. "Which one would you post today, under your business name?" (Why? If none,
   why not?)
2. "Is there anything in any of them that looks off, fake or embarrassing?"
3. *(Tell them which one is AI.)* "Does knowing it's AI change whether you'd
   post it?"
4. "How do you think your customers would react if they knew?"
5. "If FluxMuse made videos like your favourite every week, what would that be
   worth to you a month?" Let them name a number first. Don't quote a plan
   price until they have.
6. *(Only if they didn't film C:)* "What stops you filming yourself?"

Record "Owner would post?" for each video on the **Videos** tab.

### Days 8, 11, 14: posting
Each owner posts the videos they're willing to post, one format at a time,
3 days apart, on the same channel at the same time of day. Rotate which format
goes first; the **Brands** tab gives each business its posting order.

**Posting reminder (send the evening before each post):**

> Hi `[[NAME]]`, tomorrow's video is #`[[N]]`. Post it on your `[[Status / page]]` around `[[time]]`, with this caption: `[[CAPTION]]` and this link: `[[LINK]]`. Could you send me a screenshot once it's up? Thanks!

- **Format A on Facebook or TikTok:** switch on the platform's "AI-generated"
  label.
- **No paid ads in this test.**

### 72 hours after each post: measure
Fill in the **Videos** tab:
- **Posted:** Y/N, with date and time.
- **Views:** from the owner's screenshot.
- **Conversations:** search Live Chat for the video's code (e.g. `AV03A`).
- **Orders:** orders that came from those conversations.
- **Comments:** any comment calling it AI, fake or a scam. Paraphrase it, with
  no names.

If an owner says customers reacted badly, record it on the **Brands** tab
straight away. That alone can fail the trust gate.

---

## Week 3: decide

### Day 17 (after the last 72-hour window)
1. Check the **Gates** tab. Each gate reads PASS, FAIL or NEED DATA.
2. Write the decision in the kit (§7) using its "what to do with the result"
   rules. Record who decided and when.
3. **Thank-you message to every owner**, whatever they did:

> Thanks so much for testing these with us, `[[NAME]]`. Your feedback changed what we'll build. The videos are yours to keep and use however you like. `[[If relevant: here's what we learned from yours: one line]]`.

4. **Delete** any product photos you don't need any more. Keep the anonymised
   sheet.

---

## Rules that apply every day
- **Outreach:** never bulk-message, never use auto-DMs, never cold-call with AI.
- **What FluxMuse is:** never call the videos a feature people will get, and
  never mention upgrading during the test. For businesses that aren't
  FluxMuse users, this is a demo on their own products, not a trial.
- **What the avatar may say:** it speaks as the brand's presenter, never as a
  customer. No "I bought this".
- **Customer data:** no customer names or numbers in the sheet or in
  screenshots.

# FluxMuse pitch decks: guide

Four 16:9 PowerPoint decks for selling FluxMuse, all built from one script. Facts come from
`../08_Prospects/CURRENT_OFFER.md` (the source of truth) and `../00_FACTS_AND_ASSUMPTIONS.md`. Every slide has a
60–120-word talk track in the speaker notes, and the notes name the section they rely on.

| File | Slides | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| `FluxMuse_Brand_Pitch_Deck.pptx` | 26 (21 core + 5 appendix) | The master. Mixed or unknown audiences, events, and as the source to copy slides from. |
| `FluxMuse_Pitch_Solo_Entrepreneurs.pptx` | 14 (12 core + 2 FAQ) | Founder-run businesses of 1–5 people. Leads with Nano R149 and Micro R289; Founding Member R104 / R202. Short close. |
| `FluxMuse_Pitch_SMEs.pptx` | 21 (17 core + 4 appendix) | Businesses with 5–200 staff. Starter R499 / Growth R1,999 / Scale R4,999. Adds a platform slide and a "tools & team" slide. |
| `FluxMuse_Partner_Programme_Deck.pptx` | 21 (17 core + 4 appendix) | Agencies, freelancers and resellers managing 5–50 clients. Agency R9,999 list, partner wholesale R6,999/mo, illustrative economics, onboarding, co-marketing. No Founding Member offer. |

Corporate (R6,999) and Custom are sold by conversation: use the SME deck plus a proposal.

## Which deck when

- **Don't know yet?** Open with the master and ask the questions in slide 1's notes. Jump to the matching segment slide (9, 10 or 11).
- **Solo owner-operator, sells on WhatsApp, Instagram or Facebook:** Solo deck.
- **Has staff, several tools, or several brands or locations:** SME deck.
- **Manages marketing for clients:** Partner Programme deck. Never show agencies Founding Member prices.
- **Prospect outside South Africa:** paid sign-up is South Africa only. Nigeria, Kenya and Ghana are priced but not on sale. Show no prices; share `[[WAITLIST LINK]]`. The regional price sheet (`assets/infographics/pricing-regional.png`) is internal/investor-only and is not in these decks.

## Slide swap guide

- **Tailoring the master:** keep one segment slide (9, 10 or 11) and hide the other two. Hide 12 (partner model) and A2 for non-agencies. Hide 15 (case study template) if you're short on time.
- **Instagram- or Facebook-first prospects:** keep slide 6 and say plainly that Facebook and Instagram auto-posting is being switched on (Meta permissions not approved). Until then, posts are forwarded to WhatsApp Status.
- **Demo:** build a demo shop from the prospect's own public product photos and show it live (approved; it is not a trial). The old screenshot slide is gone: the site and demo-store screenshots still show a "Start free trial" button and a card payment through the demo store.
- **When checkout through FluxMuse is tested with real money:** update slides 3, 5 and 7 and FAQ "How do my customers pay?", and only then use the fee wording in CURRENT_OFFER.md §2.
- **When the Founding Member promotion closes:** remove slide 18, the Founding Member column on slide 17, and the matching lines on the plan and segment slides.
- **Once a first-group business consents to a case study:** replace slide 15 with it. Never show projected results.
- **Mixing slides across decks:** copy from the master (Reuse Slides / Keep source formatting). Speaker notes in the variant decks are tailored to that audience, so re-read them after copying.

## Rules (from CURRENT_OFFER.md)

- **Prices (ZAR/month, what you pay, no VAT added or mentioned):** Small: Free R0, Nano R149, Micro R289. Medium: Starter R499, Growth R1,999, Scale R4,999. Enterprise: Corporate R6,999, Agency R9,999, Custom on request. Annual = 10× monthly. Never quote per-plan AI credit numbers.
- **No trials, no free month.** Every paid plan starts with payment. Free is a permanent plan (free forever, no card); don't lead with it.
- **Founding Member:** live now, South African sign-ups, 30% off the first two monthly bills (Nano R104, Micro R202, Starter R349, Growth R1,399, Scale R3,499). A discount, not a free period. No end date: say "while it lasts". No annual bonus months, badges or other perks until confirmed. Never for the Agency tier.
- **Partners:** Agency tier R9,999 list; partner wholesale R6,999/mo (30% off; R69,990/yr). The R23,001 spread (20 clients × R1,500 = R30,000 − R6,999) is always labelled illustrative. Never quote a fixed margin percentage.
- **Lead guarantee:** 3 qualified leads in 30 days of go-live; if fewer, and the business shared its link and posted at least weekly, extended support at no extra charge until 3 arrive. Always with its conditions. Not a refund; there is no money-back guarantee.
- **No pilot, no customers, no social proof.** No customer counts, ratings, testimonials or results. The honest angle: a small first group of businesses, set up by hand.
- **Capabilities:** live features are "newly launched", never "proven". Checkout through FluxMuse, Facebook/Instagram auto-posting, AI Voice (beta), daily digest, click-to-WhatsApp ads and store sync are "being switched on". Instagram DMs/comments and X posting are not available.
- **Payments:** Paystack live in South Africa; Yoco and Ozow run FluxMuse's own subscription billing. pawaPay and Fincra are contracted, accounts pending. Nigeria, Kenya and Ghana are not on sale. Never mention SnapScan, Stitch, Flutterwave, PayFast or M-Pesa direct.
- **Trust claims:** verified Meta Tech Provider, official WhatsApp Cloud API, consent-only broadcasts (POPIA-aligned), row-level security, encrypted tokens, data export and deletion. If asked for certifications or a DPA, follow up in writing.
- **Accessibility:** no white text on Flux Orange (use Night or Ink); orange text on white uses Deep Orange #C24E00; body text 14pt minimum.
- **Fonts:** Poppins (headlines) and Inter (body). Install both before presenting or editing.

## Rebuilding

Edit `../_build/brand_deck/build_deck.py` (not the .pptx files) so that all four decks stay consistent, then:

```bash
cd docs/go-to-market/_build/brand_deck
python3 build_deck.py   # needs python-pptx + Pillow; embeds the current images from ../../assets
export CHROME_PATH=/opt/pw-browsers/chromium-1194/chrome-linux/chrome
python3 qa_deck.py      # notes, fonts, overlaps, CURRENT_OFFER §5 forbidden phrases, lead-guarantee conditions,
                        # headless-Chrome preview in ./preview
```
